Michael Gertz has been a professor at the [Institute of Computer Science](https://www.ifi.uni-heidelberg.de/) at [Heidelberg University](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en) since August 2008, where he leads the Data Science research group. In the 80s, he studied Computer Science at the [TU Dortmund University](https://www.tu-dortmund.de/en/). After earning his doctorate in Computer Science from [Leibniz University Hannover](https://www.uni-hannover.de/) in 1996 and spending one more year in Hannover as a postdoc, he held a professorship at the [Department of Computer Science](https://cs.ucdavis.edu/) at the [University of California, Davis (USA)](https://www.ucdavis.edu/), from 1997 until 2008.

His interdisciplinary research focuses on data science, information retrieval, chatbots, and conversational AI—often within the context of projects in medicine or law. From 2018 to 2025, he served as co-spokesperson for the Helmholtz Information & Data Science School for Health ([HIDSS4Health](https://www.hidss4health.de/)). Since March 2025, he has been a member of the [Expert Commission](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-initiative/nfdi/members) for the National Research Data Infrastructure ([NFDI](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-initiative/nfdi)) at the German Research Foundation (DFG). He has also served on Heidelberg University’s [Knowledge and Technology Transfer Advisory Board](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en/newsroom/universitat-heidelberg-new-transfer-advisory-board-strengthens-exchange-between-research-and-society) since 2025. He is a co-founder of the AI ​​startup [kmapp.io](https://kmapp.io) (established in 2022), which focuses on knowledge management for companies across various industries.
