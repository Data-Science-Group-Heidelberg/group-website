# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-calendar-days" %}Course Offerings in the Winter Semester 2026/27

Here is some important information about the lecture, the seminar, and the practicals offered by our group in the winter semester 2026/27:

<div class="course-box" markdown="1">
<p class="course-box-title">{% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-book-open-reader" %}Vorlesung "Einführung in die Praktische Informatik (IPI)"</p>

Bitte registrieren Sie sich über [heiCo](https://heico.uni-heidelberg.de/heiCO/) für die Vorlesung __und__ die Übungen. Über die Mitteilungsfunktion in heiCo werden dann kurz vor der ersten Vorlesung weitere Informationen zur Vorlesung mitgeteilt, u.a. der Zugang zu der [Moodle](https://moodle.uni-heidelberg.de/my/)-Seite zur Vorlesung.

Die Zeiten und Räume für die Übungen stehen erst ab Mitte September fest.

</div>

<div class="course-box" markdown="1">
<p class="course-box-title">{% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-comments" %}Seminar "Agentic AI for Problem Solving"</p>

The registration for the first session of the seminar (Bachelorseminar und Seminar, IS) is available via [Moodle](https://moodle.uni-heidelberg.de/user/index.php?id=32737); you can obtain the enrollment key for this Moodle course via [heiCo](https://heico.uni-heidelberg.de/heiCO/wbLv.wbShowLVDetail?pStpSpNr=426650). In the first session on Thursday, October 15, 2026, at 9:15am, we will provide more information about this seminar, the topics to be covered, and in particular how to apply for one of the 14 slots we have in this seminar. The first session will be held online as there are typically many more students interested in a seminar than there are slots. Information about the link to the online session will be posted via the forum for this seminar in Moodle. Students who get a slot for the seminar later also need to enroll via heiCo.
</div>

<div class="course-box" markdown="1">
<p class="course-box-title">{% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-laptop-code" %}Practicals "Generative AI in Practice"</p>

Information about both beginners and advanced practicals our group offers in the winter semester 2026/27 will be presented in a single session online on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at 2:15pm. You can find the link to this session in the respective course description in heiCo.
In the first session, we will present the topics for practicals and how to apply for a practical. Please note that we typically get many more applications than we have practicals to offer. In the winter semester, we expect to offer about 6-8 topics and practicals, respectively.
</div>
